1Personal and classroom use

You may use the artwork for your own crafting and in your own classroom, provided the finished projects are not shared, passed on or sold. This section attaches no further conditions to that kind of use, so go ahead and make something lovely.

This licence allows you to place PixelPetal Designs artwork inside secure, printable or downloadable teaching resources that you sell or give away, either on your own teacher-author blog or website, or on multi-seller education marketplaces such as Teachers Pay Teachers, Lehrermarktplatz / Eduki, TES and Mieux Enseigner, as well as on Etsy. Distribution anywhere else, whether free or paid, is not permitted. That includes, without limitation, Amazon, eBay and any app store.
  • Substantial educational content. Each resource must contain a meaningful amount of your own educational material and your own writing. The artwork should support your work rather than make up most of it. A few exceptions exist for early reader, vocabulary and phonics resources, described below.
  • Your own, protected document. You must build the resource yourself and deliver it in a secure format. Flatten the artwork and lock the file so that the images cannot be copied, extracted or shared from inside it.

4Printable educational resources (PDF)

Protect the artwork, and the effort you have put into your resource, by flattening the images into the page background and adding password protection to the file. Your resource must also include a meaningful amount of your own written content. Credit to PixelPetal Designs is required, in the form of a clickable link to the PixelPetal Designs website or shop (see How to give credit).

Learning activities and resources

Your resource needs original educational content with a clear teaching purpose, and a substantial amount of your own writing. Use the artwork to enrich your resource, not to form its main body. Limited exceptions for image-heavy learning tools are explained next.

Image-heavy learning tools

Early reader activities, vocabulary cards and phonics resources depend on pictures to work, and they are allowed. They must still carry educational content and a substantial amount of your own writing, for example instructions, teacher notes or suggestions for use. A resource made up of artwork alone does not qualify.

We recommend layering the artwork with other graphics, backgrounds, borders and text where suitable. This protects the images better and gives your resource a look that is truly yours.

Colouring-style resources

You may not make colouring books, colouring pages or similar resources that consist entirely or mainly of the artwork.

Colour-by-number, colour-by-letter and colour-by-code activities are permitted, as long as you add a clear educational element so the images are not left as stand-alone graphics. Include your own written content, such as student instructions, teacher notes or ideas for use.

Layering the artwork with other graphics, backgrounds, borders and text is encouraged, so that the finished resource is distinctly your own.

Classroom décor

Décor items with little or no learning value are not covered by the standard licence. Examples include classroom labels, writing paper, banners, decorations, notes to students and punch cards. If you would like to make items like these, please contact us about an extended licence.

5Paperless and digital resources

The same requirements for educational content and your own written material apply here. Credit to PixelPetal Designs is required, with a clickable link to the PixelPetal Designs website or shop.

Background images on digital platforms

You may use the artwork to build background images for digital resources on PowerPoint, Keynote, SMARTboard, Google Classroom (Google Drive and Slides) and Microsoft OneDrive. Merge the artwork with other images, shapes or text so that it becomes a secure, flattened background.

You may not distribute digital templates, editable games or other image-based products that have little or no educational content, whether free or paid. This licence does not extend to other free or paid apps, software or platforms.

Boom Learning

In Boom Learning resources you may use the artwork both as backgrounds and as unedited movable pieces, because Boom keeps the images secure within the file.

Movable elements

For this purpose only, the following rules were relaxed in response to the widespread move to remote and home learning. You may create movable elements by combining the artwork with other layers, saving the combination as one PNG or JPG file, and then placing that file in your resource. Make sure nobody can lift the original artwork out of your resource. Combine it with one or both of the following:

  1. Backgrounds. Place the artwork over a patterned background shape or shapes. Plain solid colours are easy to remove and should not be used.
  2. Text overlay. Layer bold text over the artwork so that it covers at least a third of the image.

Using a patterned background and a text overlay together is the best option. It protects the artwork and helps your resource stand out.

Movable pieces may only be used in resources that you sell. They are not permitted in resources that you give away for free.

Video

You may use the artwork in videos that are part of a learning resource and in videos that promote your learning resources. Please give credit wherever possible, for example as on-screen text in the video and as a clickable link in the video description.

Digital stickers

You may not use the artwork to create digital stickers, whether they are sold or given away.

6Social media and advertising

Social media posts

You may use the artwork in social media posts for your teacher-author business, provided that:

  • you do not make or sell clip art or graphics;
  • the artwork is combined with other images and text within your post image; and
  • PixelPetal Designs is credited on the post image itself, plus a clickable link to our website or social profile in the post text where possible.

You may not:

  • use the artwork to create logos or other branding images for your business or blog;
  • use the artwork to create social media logos or profile pictures; or
  • use the artwork in any way that suggests you created it.

Store banners and advertising images

You may use the artwork to make banners and advertising images for your teacher-author store, provided that:

  • you do not make or sell clip art or graphics;
  • the artwork is combined with other images and text within your image; and
  • PixelPetal Designs is credited where possible.

You may not use the artwork to create logos or other business branding images, or use it in any way that suggests you created it.

Product previews and cover images

You may use the artwork to enhance the previews and covers of your teacher-author products, as long as the preview or cover also shows your actual resource, for example through photographs or screenshots. The artwork may not make up most of a preview or cover.

You may not use the artwork to create previews, covers or promotional images for your own clip art or other graphics.

Pinterest pin images

You may use the artwork to make Pinterest pin images for your resources, as long as each pin shows your resource (as photographs or screenshots) and the same artwork is used inside that resource.

The artwork may not make up most of a pin image, and you may not make pins for products that do not contain that artwork. You may not upload or pin the individual illustrations themselves.

Educational blogs and websites

You may use the artwork to create images for your personal educational blog or website, except for logos and business branding images. Each image must meet all of these conditions:

  • the artwork is combined and layered with other images or text;
  • the image is flattened onto a coloured background, not white, with no transparency;
  • the image is reduced in size; and
  • the resolution is reduced to a maximum of 100 dpi.

Credit is required as a clickable logo or text link to the PixelPetal Designs website, placed in the blog post, sidebar or footer.

You may not post full-size artwork, or isolated or individual pieces of artwork, on your blog. You may not use the artwork to create blog logos, blog themes, or graphics and templates that you distribute or resell.
