# Review notes (not part of the website)

Paraphrasing does not make a legal document original in a legal sense, nor does it make it enforceable. These terms were written for a different business; have a qualified professional review them for PixelPetal Designs.

## Ambiguities in the reference page (kept as stated, not resolved)
1. **"No restrictions" vs. single-user licence.** Personal/classroom use is described as unrestricted, yet elsewhere nothing may be shared or gifted. I wrote "no further conditions under this section".
2. **"Credit where possible"** (banners, video) vs. mandatory credit elsewhere. "Where possible" is undefined.
3. **"Significant amount" of own content** is never quantified.
4. **Movable elements** carried a March 2020 pandemic-era "update" note. I kept the rule and dropped the dated wording. Confirm you want the same relaxation.
5. **"Extended licence for classroom décor"** exists on the reference shop. I point to contacting you; only keep it if you offer one.
6. **Craft activities / colouring pages ("Colour In" range)** are specific to the reference brand. Remove or adjust if you have no such products.
7. **Governing law.** The reference cites UK and international law plus the DMCA. I wrote "applicable national and international copyright law, including the DMCA". Name your actual jurisdiction.
8. **Named marketplaces** (Teachers Pay Teachers, Eduki, TES, Mieux Enseigner, Etsy) and the excluded ones (Amazon, eBay, app stores) are copied as permissions/limits; confirm they suit your business.
9. **Product types** (clip art, stamps, papers, overlays, alphabets) should match what you actually sell.
10. The reference mentions "password protecting" PDFs and "flattened and locked" files; both are kept.
